# The AI-referred arrival

Every engagement check is evaluated against this arrival, not against a homepage visit.

The visitor:
- lands on a deep page, not the homepage
- has no navigation history and no session
- was told something specific about this page by an assistant and arrives expecting it
- carries prior context the site knows nothing about (Appendix E)
- decides within one screen whether this is the right place

The four questions, in order:
1. Whose page is this? (E-ORIENT)
2. Is the thing I was promised actually here, and can I see it now? (E-ANSWER)
3. Where does this sit in the wider site? (E-ORIENT)
4. What can I do next without starting over? (E-CONTINUE)

E-ANSWER-04 is the join between the two halves of the brief: the probe found the fact
in the text, and we measure whether the rendered page puts that fact where an arriving
human can actually see it.

Not in scope: aesthetics, brand voice, colour, imagery quality, CTA styling.
