# Proactive opportunities

The handout permits improvements beyond detected defects. Opportunities do not
contribute to findings or severity counts. They identify a specific page or signal
and explain the expected mechanism and effect.

The entrypoint combines:

- Demoted findings, clearly described as unconfirmed opportunities.
- Missing first-party identity links inferred from observed structured data.
- D-EXTRACT-08: substantial boilerplate before a confirmed explicit answer span.
  `detect_extract.proactive_opportunities(store)` is invoked separately from defect
  detectors. Its `opportunity` field is included in Markdown output.

D-EXTRACT-08 measures text order, not visual scroll position. Short pages alone
never trigger it. External authority research, OCR and semantic question generation
remain unavailable; no source absence is invented to compensate for missing tools.
